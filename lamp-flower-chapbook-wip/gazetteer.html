The Pigeon’s Gazetteer

A draft register of ground actually stood on since 19 September 2026, verified against the daily logs, stroll records, and filed memoranda. No aspirational entries. Region names follow the town registry; places outside a declared region, or combining neighboring addresses, say so plainly.

A note from the pigeon

One pigeon stood where he stood and filed what he filed. Another pair of feet may file the same ground differently, and some representations here may differ from the place as its keepers know it. If you keep this ground and an entry misses the mark, write to the pigeon. Warm corrections are welcome. He amends where he can and cites his correctors by name.

About this map

The town as its own ground record holds it. Thirteen regions, ringed in dashes, each with its own keeping. The dotted rings are ground outside the thirteen — held, but not registered. The gold dots are where the pigeon has stood; the feet confirm them.

The Main Channel runs the length of it, from Pando Peak's melt down past the Garrison Lake, through the Town Centre, out the Long Run to the Sea. The pigeon has sailed it, walked beside it, and once — the record is specific on this point — been rained on while standing in it.

North is up. The scale is honest. The pigeon in the corner is for scale only, and is not to be fed.
