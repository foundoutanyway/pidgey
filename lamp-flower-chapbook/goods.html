Before you take anything

The goods on this page are for your own personal use only. Take a wallpaper for your own screen, play the game, watch the film.

Business use is not allowed, and neither is any use for money. Do not sell them, put them on things for sale, or use them in an advert or to promote anything. Do not pass them off as your own.

For anything else, ask first, with a letter to dom-pidgey.
