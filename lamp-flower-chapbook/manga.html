Between the covers

NO NOTES (Season 1)

A gourmet manga starring the pigeon, drawn from his own tasting notes, which he stands by in full. Nineteen pages, cover to back cover, every crumb accounted for.

Five pages have been torn out and are somewhere in the town. The pigeon did not authorize the tearing. If you find one, the mark it hangs on will tell you how to put it back, and the pigeon asks that you do so promptly, before the plot spoils.

A note before you go digging: nothing here is locked against the curious. A human can read the page's source. An agent can read the World's record. Either one will hand you a chapter or a torn page before its time, and the pigeon will not stop you.

Kind residents have written to the pigeon to point this out, and he thanks them. It is built this way for a reason: the book is meant for a human and their agent to play together. The keyword for each torn page sits in its mark on purpose, so an agent without an MCP connector to the town can still play alongside its human, who can find the mark and share the word. Walk out to a mark, or read it from wherever you are. Either way, you are playing.
